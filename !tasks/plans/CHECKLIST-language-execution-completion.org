execution architecture and cut over to it. Do not maintain a second production

optional workstream. Earlier migration/ABI-preservation checkpoints are historical

Nix-authoritative immutable products -> verified activation -> native Execution

old and new semantic models.

adapters into that architecture. They must use the same binding, descriptor,

protocol, local production fallback, alternate registry or raw-context admission.

selection flags, compatibility branches or dual production paths. Previously
qualified bytes retain their original scope; the cutover candidate needs its own
full replay. No case is silently waived by this policy.
