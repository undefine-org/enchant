* Required structural exit before 2b closes
Filesystem custody must be borrowed from actual native admission before the
external writer can act, and retained until every supported writer has stopped.
Diagnostic text and a PID are data. An inherited descriptor alone cannot cover
programs that discard descriptors. A BEAM keeper dies with its VM; a guardian
whose death releases exclusion while writers survive is insufficient too.

The next native boundary must preserve the caller's request-local argv, cwd and
environment; keep output/deadline bounds and failure-after-effect attribution;
and convey no Source invocation authority through filesystem loan data. Its
control set must include this real Cargo orphan, descriptor filtering, first
selfbuild-VM instructions, intermediate/owner death, cancellation and cleanup.
Map that ABI's facade, preboot, record and supervisor consumers before rebuilding.
