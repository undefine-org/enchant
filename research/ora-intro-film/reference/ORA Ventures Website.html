The company of tomorrow

AI will become part of how every company is organised.

Most teams already use AI. Each person has their own tools, their own instructions and their own habits. The gains are real, yet they stay individual, uneven and difficult to govern.

The next step is organisational. Companies will manage AI the way they manage finance or information systems: as shared infrastructure, with one source of knowledge, common methods and clear rules on data.

That transition is a matter of architecture and sequencing. We take care of both, step by step, and we remain accountable for the result.

Personal assistants→A shared company layer
Knowledge in individual chats→One governed source
Quality that depends on the user→Output held to company standards
Data leaving unnoticed→Data kept where you decide
Two ways to use AI

The difference is in the foundations.

Two companies can use the same AI models and get very different results. What separates them is whether their assistants share the company's knowledge, follow its methods and stay within its rules.

Fragmented useShared infrastructure
KnowledgeScattered across personal conversationsOne governed source, cited in every answer
MethodsRewritten by each user, every timeEncoded once in company playbooks
QualityDepends on the individualReviewed against shared standards
DataHard to traceStored and processed where you decide
ImprovementStays with the person who found itMeasured and shared across teams
EfficiencyClose to zero, sometimes negative when errors have to be fixedAbove 100 %, consistent across the team
Five ways we work with you

From first assessment to running infrastructure.

The five steps follow the natural order of an implementation. You can start with any of them, and each one delivers value on its own.

01
Assess

Understand where AI creates value

We review your processes, data and tools with your management team. We identify where AI saves time or improves quality, and where it introduces risk.

DeliverableA prioritised roadmap with costs and expected impact
02
Train

Prepare leaders and teams

Practical sessions built on your own cases. Leaders learn to make informed decisions about AI. Teams learn to delegate tasks, guide assistants and check their work.

DeliverableTrained teams and internal usage guidelines
03
Connect

Upgrade the tools you already use

We connect AI to your CRM, email, documents and business software. Your procedures, vocabulary and standards are written into shared playbooks that every assistant follows.

DeliverableConnected tools and a first library of company playbooks
04
Build

Develop what the market does not offer

When no existing software fits the way you work, we design and build it with you. You own the code, the data and the methods.

DeliverableCustom applications and agents, documented and handed over
05
Host & operate

Run it on infrastructure you control

Private deployment, self-hosted applications and local models where confidentiality requires it. Optimisers monitor quality over time and improve the playbooks as your teams use them.

DeliverableA secure platform, monitored and improved over time

Every engagement is scoped with you. We agree on the objective, the timeline and how success is measured before any work starts.

What clients ask us first.

Do we need a technical team?

No. We handle the technical work and train your teams to use what we build. A single point of contact on your side is enough.

Where does our data go?

Where you decide: a provider you choose under your own contract, or servers you control. We document every flow.

Where should we start?

Usually with an assessment or a training session. Both give you a clear view of priorities before any larger investment.

Do we have to choose between a product and a custom project?

No. Our products run on the same foundations as our custom work, and most projects combine the two.

Do you work outside Morocco?

Yes. We work in French and English, on site in Morocco and remotely elsewhere.
