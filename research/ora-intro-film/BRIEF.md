# The Common Block — shot-by-shot brief

**Master:** 45 s · 16:9 · 1920×1080 · 60 fps · 128 BPM (96 beats) · no voiceover.
**Only copy:** "ORA Ventures" in the closing lockup.
**Story in one line:** many private AI lights become one shared foundation, and the
company that builds it is the foundation itself.
**Emotional arc:** recognition → friction → revelation → relief → momentum →
confidence → scale → ownership.

[Visual storyboard](storyboard/index.html) · [Rendered proof of shot 03](proof/README.md) ·
[Research](RESEARCH.md) · [Alternative directions](DIRECTIONS.md)

## The visual contract

- **One set, continuous camera.** Site grid → tower façade → rooms → underground → back up through the tower → city → aerial → site grid. No cuts between unrelated renders. Every transition is a camera move or a match on a shape.
- **The orb** is each employee's assistant. Fragmented: each orb has its own hue, pulse period and brightness. Shared: one cream orb colour, one pulse locked to the bar, a coral thread down to the block.
- **The crystal** is the output. Fragmented: four different cuts, one cracks. Shared: one identical cut, well lit.
- **The common block** is the playbooks layer, the only coral object. It has engraved lines like rows of text (procedures) and glows on the beat. It is the O in the final lockup.
- **Gauge columns** are thin light columns inside each room. Height is efficiency. Colour follows the site's code: red below the floor, amber, grey, green past a dashed 100 % mark. Never show numbers.
- **Palette** is the site's own (see `RESEARCH.md`). Navy night, cream light, coral for the company layer only.
- **Type:** Schibsted Grotesk for the lockup, nothing else on screen.

## Shot plan

### 01 — The page becomes a city · 0:00–0:05

- **Frame:** the website's navy hero grid, face-on, exactly as on the page.
- **Action:** for half a second it is just the page. Then the camera tilts and the grid gets depth: each cell becomes a window of a tower at dusk. Inside each cell, a small orb, each a different colour and pulsing at its own rate.
- **Camera:** a slow tilt from orthographic face-on to a 3/4 low angle up the façade.
- **Viewer understands:** every person in this company already has their own AI.
- **Sound:** 5–7 unrelated soft pulses at different tempos. Polyrhythmic, slightly uncomfortable.
- **Exit:** push into one window.
- **Gate:** tower instancing (≈ 54 rooms) and per-instance orb clocks.

### 02 — Everyone on their own · 0:05–0:12

- **Frame:** a slide along one floor; five rooms read left to right.
- **Action:** each figure's orb produces a crystal, and each cut differs. In room 3 the crystal cracks red and the figure leans over to fix it. The same document tile sits in every room, each copy subtly different. Thin coral threads drift out through the windows and fade into the night. Each room's gauge column hovers low and amber. Room 3's dips below the floor in red.
- **Camera:** a lateral track at desk height, with one rack to the cracked crystal.
- **Viewer understands:** scattered knowledge, uneven quality, data leaving unnoticed, efficiency near zero.
- **Sound:** the polyrhythm thickens. A dry crack at the fracture. A faint breathy tone each time a thread leaves.
- **Exit:** the camera drops through the floor, following a falling thread.
- **Gate:** crystal variants, a crack event, thread curves leaving bounds, and gauge columns driven by the score.

### 03 — The difference is in the foundations · 0:12–0:19 · **RENDERED PROOF**

- **Frame:** below street level, on the same navy grid laid flat.
- **Action:**
  - A shadow grows on the grid before anything is visible. The data foundation lands on beat 3, then the connectors on beat 6.
  - The coral common block hovers in from above, hesitates, and commits on beat 10. A coral shockwave runs through the grid.
  - The scattered motes snap from their own paths into three rings at one tempo.
  - Five assistant cells land, then five team tiles.
  - Coral lines run along the block and up to every team, lighting each tile.
- **Camera:** a low dolly back, a rising crane, then a 3/4 hero orbit.
- **Viewer understands:** something shared and structural sits underneath the work.
- **Sound:** silence before the first landing, then thuds on the beat. The core lands with a sub-heavy slam and a chord. One tick per team that lights up.
- **Exit:** follow the central coral line upward, through the ceiling into the tower.
- **Status:** `proof/evidence/layers.mp4` is this shot at 15 s (website cut). The 7 s master version keeps the same landings and the same order, re-timed so the core lands on the master's beat 30.

### 04 — One layer reaches everyone · 0:19–0:25

- **Frame:** the full tower in section, seen from slightly below.
- **Action:**
  - Coral lines rise from the block through every floor, reaching each room in sequence on the beat.
  - As a line arrives, that room's orb takes the cream colour and locks to the shared pulse, and its crystal re-forms into the shared cut.
  - All gauge columns climb together, cross the dashed 100 % mark and turn green.
- **Camera:** a vertical crane up the tower, rising with the lines.
- **Viewer understands:** one shared layer gives consistent output and efficiency above 100 %.
- **Sound:** the polyrhythm collapses into the 128 BPM pulse. The harmonic bed opens up.
- **Gate:** line routing through the tower instances, the orb sync transition, and gauges crossing 100 %.

### 05 — One improvement, everywhere · 0:25–0:30

- **Frame:** a room in the upper middle, with the tower in context.
- **Action:** that room's figure cuts a better crystal (a new facet). A bright pulse runs *down* its line into the common block. The engraved lines on the block re-flow, briefly visible. The pulse spreads back *up* all lines, and every room's crystal gains the new facet at once.
- **Camera:** a quick vertical whip down with the pulse and back up. Under a second each way.
- **Viewer understands:** optimisers mean an improvement found once reaches every team.
- **Sound:** a rising tone down, a resolved chord up, and the melody's inversion.
- **Gate:** pulse travel along existing lines, the block's engraving update, and a facet morph.

### 06 — Built to be trusted · 0:30–0:36

- **Frame:** three rooms close up.
- **Action:**
  - The coral data threads that leaked in shot 02 now curve back down into the foundation and never leave the building.
  - Every crystal trails a thin thread to the document tile it came from.
  - Before a crystal leaves the room, the figure's hand settles on it. Only then does it move on.
- **Camera:** a slow lateral drift. The trust beat deserves a calm camera.
- **Viewer understands:** data stays yours · every answer has a source · people keep the decision.
- **Sound:** sparse. Each hand-touch is a soft wooden knock.
- **Gate:** thread routing into the foundation, source tiles, and the hand gesture. "No lock-in" is deliberately left to the page.

### 07 — A city on the same foundation · 0:36–0:41

- **Frame:** a hard pullback to a dusk skyline over the Atlantic.
- **Action:** the coral foundation line runs under the street and up into three new buildings, which light up in turn:
  - a library of stacked folios (Knowledger);
  - a factory floor with machine bays (Blueprint);
  - a hotel courtyard with lit balconies (Backdesk).
  A minaret-like spire and the sea at the horizon place it in Casablanca without a postcard.
- **Camera:** a fast pullback with a slow settle, keeping velocity from shot 06.
- **Viewer understands:** there are ready-made products for specific sectors.
- **Sound:** the full arrangement, at its widest.
- **Gate:** three massing models in the same materials as the tower. **Keep them abstract.** Detailed buildings tip into illustration.

### 08 — ORA · 0:41–0:45

- **Frame:** straight-down aerial view.
- **Action:** as the camera reaches top-down, the streets straighten into the site's 48 px grid. The coral common block, seen from above, becomes a ring: the **O**. "RA Ventures" sets beside it in cream Schibsted Grotesk. One cream orb keeps orbiting the O. Hold from 0:42.4 to the end.
- **Viewer understands:** the company is the foundation underneath everything they just saw.
- **Sound:** everything drops out except one pulse, then one sustained note.
- **Gate:** a wordmark from shaped outlines, the top-down camera handoff, and a pixel-match of the final frame to the site hero grid.

## Sound design

An original score on the BL event clock at 128 BPM, synthesised by the bounded
worker as in MAKE WORLDS and the proof. The structure follows the story:
polyrhythm (fragmented) → silence → beat-locked landings → one shared pulse → full
arrangement → one pulse → one note. No stock music, no voice.

## Review gates before full production

1. **Silent grey-box animatic** of all 8 shots at final timing, using box stand-ins on the proof's renderer. Approve the story before materials.
2. **Type:** package Schibsted Grotesk (OFL) and test the lockup against the site hero.
3. **Readability test, muted:** show the animatic to someone who hasn't seen the site and ask what the company does. "Shared AI infrastructure for companies" means pass.
4. **Full-motion review of every shot at 1080p60.** Selected-frame review is not motion QA.
5. Confirm with ORA that the illustrative gauge story (efficiency above 100 %) is acceptable as visual rhetoric. The site itself marks the values as illustrative.
