#+begin_principles
Commit before checking | A checkpoint makes interruptions and integrations recoverable. A test run uses a frozen snapshot.
Keep the pin | Blueprint source edits use its qualified SDK. A newly built Enchant SDK is a separate adoption decision backed by checks.
Observe one job | Follow the live handle to its terminal result. Preserve failures; do not rerun unchanged setup because a session lost context.
Use a durable gate | AUTO_RELEASE_ENABLED defaults off. Manual/tag release checks still require the exact successful runtime; no endless publication-cancellation loop.
Budget the actual work | One substantive build/check at a time, bounded by the repository's existing systemd/Nix limits. No routine stress or memory-pressure experiments.
#+end_principles
