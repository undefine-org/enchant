* Research questions: investigate the design as a whole
1. What should be the smallest coherent application vocabulary? Compare at least
   three serious designs, including a minimal extension of existing values/APIs,
   a declarative feature/capability design, and a compiler-checked role/evidence
   design. Do not predetermine the winner from the supplied documents.
2. How should Datom, authorized query/pull, bounded paging/search/counts, immutable
   read models, derived views, cache invalidation and clocks compose? Which work
   can be admitted structurally, bounded natively, proved symbolically or only
   measured? Handle joins, selective filters and incomplete/refused outcomes.
3. How should live.app/socket/projection/temporal, Loom components/forms/tokens,
   routes, typed commands, private drafts, attempt identity, SSR and client
   controllers meet without duplicated state/authorization/schema authorities?
4. How do ownership, supervision, publication, reload generations, native
   execution and source-free packaging fit the same model? Cover startup,
   acquisition failures, borrowed capabilities, reconnect, cancellation and
   uncertain provider effects.
5. Study typed, codebase/contextual phase/boundary/refactor, system IR/source/
   summary/analyze/arity/typecheck/footprint/evidence/incr/publication/execution/
   suspension/ownership/linear/place, and proc contracts. Identify real integration
   seams, gaps, circular dependencies and redundant representations.
6. Study Datalog least fixpoints, system.gfp/finite liveness, SMT/ANF translation,
   Z3/oracle/pool/decision ladder, miniKanren synthesis, Veritas properties/models/
   ledger, deodorant/rewrite/span preservation and Live lint. Allocate each a
   precise job and define coverage/trust/unknown semantics. Do not call every
   problem a solver problem or build a duplicate effect/type/proof system.
7. Could one compiler-produced application/feature description drive routes,
   policy, reads, intents, presentation schema, ownership, deployment and tests
   without becoming a central monolith or annotation theatre? Compare explicit
   values/composition with macros and inference; preserve escape hatches honestly.
8. What does a new application author actually write? Show realistic contrasting
   examples: a tiny CRUD app, multi-tenant search/list/detail app, hotel-style
   external-effect workflow, long-running job and private/offline-capable draft.
   Show positive examples plus rejected, unknown and unsupported cases.
9. What should developer tooling/MCP expose incrementally? Avoid whole-corpus
   initialization and repeated parsing. Explain proof/evidence provenance,
   dependency invalidation, exact identities, source/compiled agreement and cost.
10. What should become ordinary defaults in public scaffolds versus opt-in strict
    profiles? Identify an incremental migration and a qualification story for
    two independent consumers and a source-free SDK. Do not force the parent
    Backdesk work to wait for any design or upstream feature.
