* Reporting template and stopping conditions
For each new execution retain:
- Claim and matrix ID; expected observable result and a working positive control.
- Exact source/runtime/package/schema/policy/dataset and fixture hashes, dirty
  source state, target/backend, resource limits, clock, engine/model version.
- Commands/drivers, safe stdout/stderr, status, coverage including omissions,
  actual work/latency/allocation/output counters, seed/witness/shrunk replay.
- Cleanup outcome and any background work still draining; never kill unrelated VMs.

Stop a probe on its ceiling and retain =timeout/cancelled/unknown=; do not relabel
it failed correctness without a counterexample. Stop continuation after cumulative
budget/no progress. A fixed page growth run reports exact/incomplete/refused side
by side. Do not retry until green without preserving every earlier outcome.
For performance use a fixed declared workload and sufficient samples before
percentiles; warm p95 needs at least the proposed 100 samples, separate from first
use/cold startup. Track browser applied/paint, not only server enqueue time.
