#+title: Source designs retained for unification

These are exact snapshots of the untracked design documents preserved during
the worktree cleanup. =source-manifest.json= records their content hashes.

=actor-capabilities.org= proposes capability/typestate agreements and an upload
service prototype. Its examples and acceptance criteria describe future work.
The original .bl.org filename is recorded by the cleanup archive; this reference
uses .org so it is kept as source material rather than a runnable livebook.

=efficiency-rules/= contains the original 26-file application-design study,
supplied performance inputs and contextual reports. Their dates, source hashes,
evidence limitations and research status remain authoritative for those claims.
Paths and links inside them refer to the source checkouts inspected at that time.
They do not qualify current main or imply the proposed APIs are implemented.

The active reviews are =../UNIFICATION.bl.org= and =../DATOM.bl.org=.
